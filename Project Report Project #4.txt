Project Objective:

The objective of this project is to analyze supply chain operations to identify key business insights related to sales performance, profitability, customer segments, shipping efficiency, product performance, and regional sales. The dashboard helps business managers monitor KPIs and make data-driven decisions to improve operational efficiency and profitability.

Project Workflow:
1. Data Collection
Imported the DataCo Supply Chain Dataset.
Explored dataset structure and attributes.

2. Data Cleaning (Python)
Performed data preprocessing using Pandas.

Cleaning Steps:
Loaded dataset using Pandas.
Explored dataset information.
Checked missing values.
Checked duplicate records.
Removed unnecessary columns.
Filled missing values in customer-related fields.
Removed the Order Zipcode column due to excessive missing values.
Saved cleaned dataset as CSV for further analysis.

Business Insights:

Sales Performance:
Certain regions contribute significantly more revenue than others.
Product sales are concentrated among a few high-performing products.

Customer Analysis:
One customer segment generates the highest overall revenue.
Customer purchasing behavior varies across segments.

Department Performance:
Some departments contribute substantially higher profits.
Profitability differs across product categories.

Shipping Analysis:
Shipping modes have different average delivery times.
Delivery performance directly impacts operational efficiency.

Market Performance:
Sales distribution varies across international markets.
High-performing markets contribute a significant share of total revenue.

Project Outcome:

The Supply Chain Analytics Dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of business performance by combining sales, profit, shipping, customer, and product analytics into a single interactive dashboard. It enables decision-makers to monitor operational performance, identify high-performing regions and products, analyze customer behavior, and improve supply chain efficiency.

Conclusion

This project demonstrates an end-to-end data analytics workflow, including data cleaning with Python, business analysis using SQL, and interactive dashboard development in Power BI. It showcases practical analytical skills and the ability to transform raw business data into meaningful insights that support strategic decision-making.
